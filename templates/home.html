What is Meal Connect?

A seamless bridge between surplus food and those in need.

Meal Connect is an innovative food-waste management and prediction platform. We empower establishments to proactively manage their food inventory using cutting-edge machine learning, drastically reducing waste. When a surplus does occur, our platform instantly connects establishments with registered NGOs, ensuring perfectly good food reaches those who need it most.

How It Works

1. Record Daily Food

Establishments securely log their daily food production and consumption.

2. Auto-Calculate Waste

The system automatically tracks and calculates the day's total surplus.

3. ML Predicts Tomorrow

Machine learning models analyze trends to predict expected waste for upcoming days.

4. Threshold Check

If surplus exceeds defined limits, the system triggers distribution workflows.

5. Optional NGO Alert

Nearby registered NGOs are instantly notified of available food for pickup.

6. Collected & Tracked

Food is safely collected by NGOs, closing the loop with full traceability.
